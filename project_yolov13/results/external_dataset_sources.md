# External dataset source and license audit (4 October 2026)

| Dataset | Verified source facts | Relevance and use decision |
|---|---|---|
| **AdvT-shirt-1K** | The authors describe 1,131 physical-world images, nine adversarial T-shirts, varied participant counts/scenes, and separate YOLO boxes for people and patches. The Hugging Face card lists `apache-2.0`. The downloaded mirror has 1,123 images, 4,403 person boxes, and 1,548 patch boxes across five participant-count groups; the eight-image difference from the paper/repository headline remains unresolved. | Used in two separate protocols: zero-shot person-detection transfer, and a patch-only fine-tuning benchmark on a derived deterministic 70/15/15 split (seed 42, stratified within source folders). This split is not provided by the source. The patch task is an external physical-patch localization proxy, not the project's exact SpongePatch/DoS task. Sources: [Hugging Face card and license](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xingjunm/Adv-T-shirt), [authors' repository](https://github.com/Wwangb/AdvT-shirt-1K), [ICCV 2025 BadPatch paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025W/Findings/html/Wang_BadPatch_Diffusion-Based_Generation_of_Physical_Adversarial_Patches_ICCVW_2025_paper.html). |
| **APRICOT / APRICOT-Mask** | APRICOT has 1,011 physical-patch photographs and box annotations. APRICOT-Mask adds masks/revised boxes and uses dev=138 images from 6 patches, test=873 images from 54 different patches. JHU states Apache-2.0 for APRICOT-Mask. | Relevant for patch localization and unseen-patch generalization, but not the same task. Not downloaded because FTP and HTTPS requests to the host timed out. Sources: [JHU APRICOT-Mask page/license](https://aiem.jhu.edu/datasets/apricot-mask/), [APRICOT paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.08166), [CVPR 2022 paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/papers/Liu_Segment_and_Complete_Defending_Object_Detectors_Against_Adversarial_Patch_Attacks_With_Robust_Patch_Detection_CVPR_2022_paper.pdf). |
| **GAP (NAPGuard)** | The official repository reports 9,266 images, 25 patch types, bounding boxes, and 5,617/3,649 train/test images. | A larger possible corpus, but the inspected repository links to Drive and does not state a dataset license, so it was not downloaded. Source: [official NAPGuard repository](https://github.com/wsynuiag/NAPGaurd). |

## Local data handling and evaluation scope

AdvT-shirt-1K is stored at `dataset/external/adv_tshirt_1k/`. The existing person-only YAML at `dataset/adv_tshirt_person_eval.yaml` uses class 0 (`person`) for the separate COCO-pretrained zero-shot transfer benchmark. The raw snapshot's `adv_labels` folders were audited and provide patch boxes for every one of the 1,123 images: 1,548 boxes total. Two patch boxes exceed an image edge by only `5e-7` normalized units due to decimal rounding; the derived copy clips those edges to `[0,1]`. The derived patch-only split contains 785/169/169 images and 1,106/220/222 boxes for train/validation/test. Its YAML is `dataset/adv_tshirt_patch_detection.yaml`; the split and source-file mapping are recorded in `results/adv_tshirt_patch_dataset_manifest.json`. The deterministic split uses seed 42 and is stratified within the five source folders. Hugging Face download caches remain under `dataset/.cache/huggingface/`.

The original NCKH split folders remain empty. Both external experiments must be reported separately and must not be presented as task-dataset results. The mirror discrepancy (1,123 available images versus 1,131 stated by the authors) remains explicit.
